CALCULATION RULES
Row grain: one store per row (single-period snapshot). Weighted margins = Σ(Monthly_Sales × margin%) ÷ Σ(Monthly_Sales), not a simple average of percentages. Loss-making = Net_Margin_Pct < 0. Sales per employee = Σ Monthly_Sales ÷ Σ Employees. Top-10% concentration = sales of the top ⌈10%⌉ stores ÷ total sales. Correlations are Pearson r (association only, not causation). Region comparisons require ≥ 5 stores per group. Currency unit is not specified in the file, so none is shown. All figures recompute from the uploaded file and active filters.
